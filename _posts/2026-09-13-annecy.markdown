A short visual travelogue from Annecy, capturing the mountain light, sun-drenched swims, and quiet moments along the canals. I also composed the music for this short film and decided a slow ambient track best captured the rest and peace we found in the mountain air. In my art, I am increasingly looking to convey feeling over information—to transport the viewer to a memory or a distant place, and perhaps even form a connection with a stranger across space and time.
